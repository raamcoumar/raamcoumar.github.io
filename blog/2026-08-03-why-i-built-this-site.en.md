## why i built this site

i've wanted a personal site for years. something small, simple, and actually *mine* — not a linkedin profile, not a twitter thread, just a place on the internet that feels like home.

a friend gave me the codebase for his own hand-drawn, minimalist site and told me to make it my own. so i did.

the original site was built with claude code — pure html, css, and javascript. no frameworks, no build steps, no npm install. just files in a folder. that simplicity is what made it possible for me (a marketer, not a developer) to understand every line and reshape it.

here's what i changed:

- swapped the name, obviously
- rewrote the about page from scratch
- turned "works" into "career" — same mechanic, different story
- stripped the blog down and rebuilt it
- swapped out every social link and contact detail

what i kept: the hand-drawn doodles, the color theme system, the bilingual engine (even if my chinese is... questionable), and the draggable cat. some things are too good to touch.

if you're thinking about building your own site: steal this one. the `SWAP-THIS.md` file tells you exactly how. that's what it was made for.
