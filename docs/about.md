just a regular person

i like telling stories and building stuff. mostly little ideas, little experiments.

i started out as a copywriter, became a content manager, and now work as a social media brand strategist.

i love music and movies. i'm terrible at writing reviews. so the best i can offer is my letterboxd entries or "don't bother." somehow that feels more honest anyway.

i love traveling, even if i haven't been to nearly as many places as i'd like. there's a long list waiting.

some days i feel like i can figure anything out. some days everything feels like it's held together with tape and wishful thinking. both versions of me are real.

some nights i dream about the future like it's already here. some days i'm just trying to get through the day.

i saw other people writing little notes on their own websites, so i figured i'd leave a few of my own.

thanks for making it this far :)
